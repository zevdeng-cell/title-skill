# High-traffic blog title formulas

Derived from patterns across ~2000 publicly high-signal articles (major publishers and evergreen blogs). Real Google click counts were not available; treat these as **structure patterns that correlate with popular posts**, not a guarantee of traffic.

## Target shape

- **Length:** about **45–70 characters**, **7–12 words** (corpus median ~53 chars / 9 words).
- Prefer **specific outcome + audience or method**; avoid vague single nouns.
- One clear promise per title. Do not stuff every pattern into one line.

## Core formulas (use in this priority order)

### 1. Colon subtitle (most common ~1 in 4)
`[Topic or promise]: [Specific angle, format, or proof]`

Examples of shape:
- `Keyword Research: The Beginner’s Guide`
- `Link Building for SEO: Proven Strategies That Still Work`

Rules:
- Left side = searchable topic (short).
- Right side = format/benefit (`Complete Guide`, `Beginner’s Guide`, `What Still Works`, `Updated for AI Search`).

### 2. How-to (very common opener; “How” is the #1 first word)
`How to [do X] [qualifier]`

Optional qualifiers:
- step count: `in 7 Steps`
- audience: `for Beginners` / `for Small Businesses`
- proof: `(From Start to Finish)` / `[+ free templates]`
- year: `(2026)`

### 3. Numbered listicle
`[N] [Things/Tips/Strategies/Ways/Tools/Mistakes] to [outcome] [year optional]`

Rules:
- Odd numbers and mid-range counts (5–15) appear often; avoid fake precision.
- Number must match the article body.

### 4. Definition / explainers
`What Is/Are [Term]? [Benefit or next action]`

Often paired with a second clause: `& Why You Need Them`, `& How It Works (2026)`.

### 5. Guide / checklist authority frame
Include one authority word: `Complete`, `Ultimate`, `Beginner’s`, `Essential`, `Definitive`
plus a format word: `Guide`, `Checklist`, `Template`, `Playbook`, `Cheat Sheet`

Prefer **one** authority word. Stacking “Ultimate Complete Definitive” reads spammy.

### 6. Question titles (use sparingly ~6%)
`[How much / Is / Can / Should / Why / Does] … ?`

Best when the query is already phrased as a question in search.

### 7. Comparison
`[A] vs. [B]: [Which / Difference / When to choose]`

### 8. Freshness year marker (~1 in 10)
Add `(2026)`, `[2026]`, or `in 2026` when the content is annually updated (tools, best-of, SEO, shopping).

Place year at the **end** or in parentheses — not mid-phrase unless natural.

## Secondary levers

| Lever | When | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| Second person (`you` / `your`) | Practical advice | `How to Fix Your Wi-Fi Problems` |
| Power words (`actually`, `still`, `need-to-know`, `right way`) | Differentiation | `…posts people actually want to read` |
| Parenthetical aside | Scope / update / freebie | `(Updated for AI Search)`, `(+ free templates)` |
| Brand dash suffix | Publisher house style | `… - Moz`, `… \| WIRED` — omit unless writing for that brand |
| Gerund opener | Process / announcement | `Building…`, `Introducing…` — weaker for SEO intent; use carefully |

## Chinese high-traffic flavor (if writing 中文)

Observed patterns differ from English SEO titles:
- News/scoop framing: `独家｜…`、`刚刚，…`
- Em-dash or `｜` topic tags: `…｜智能涌现首发`
- Long specificity over short keyword density
- Listicle/checklist still works: `…选购思考清单｜…怎么选？`

Do not force English colon-listicle templates onto Chinese news posts.

## Generation workflow

1. Write the **search intent** in one plain sentence (who + problem + desired result).
2. Pick **one primary formula** from Core formulas (default: How-to or Colon subtitle).
3. Draft **5 title variants** using different secondary levers.
4. Score each (yes/no):
   - Specific outcome clear in under 12 words?
   - Contains a searchable head term?
   - Honest (number/year/format match the draft)?
   - No stacked spam words?
5. Ship the highest-scoring one; keep 2 runners-up for A/B or social.

## Anti-patterns (avoid)

- Keyword stuffing or exact-match spam (`Best Cheap Party Supplies Buy Online`).
- Empty superlatives with no object (`Amazing Tips You Need`).
- Misleading year or count.
- Title that promises a guide when the page is a thin list.
- Copying a publisher’s `| Brand` suffix onto your own site.

## Quick templates to fill

```
How to {outcome} {for audience} ({N} Steps)
{Topic}: The {Beginner’s|Complete} Guide ({year})
{N} {Tips|Strategies|Tools} to {outcome} in {year}
What Is {term}? {Why it matters / How it works}
{A} vs. {B}: Which Should You Choose?
Why {surprising claim} ({short proof})
```

## Output format when using this skill

Return:
1. Intent sentence
2. Chosen primary formula
3. 5 candidate titles
4. Recommended title + 1-line reason
