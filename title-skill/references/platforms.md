# Publishing Formats

Use the same supported content promise, but choose a different angle for each publishing format.

| Format | Deliverable | Useful angles | Main failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WeChat article | article title | scene, contrast, deliverable, mechanism | vague or exaggerated promise |
| Xiaohongshu note | note title | reader language, identity, negative space | searchable subject appears too late |
| X short post | first line, not a title | concrete action, scene, contrast | setup or teaser with no information |
| X article | title field | contrast, number, deliverable | long WeChat-style summary |
| Douyin | video title or description opening | consequence, curiosity, negative space | false suspense or interaction bait |
| Channels | video title or opening line | consequence, specificity | overblown short-video language |

Exact limits and truncation points change across editors, devices, and entry surfaces. Treat remembered limits as approximate; verify in the current official editor when a candidate is close to a hard limit.

## WeChat

Make the opening segment self-sufficient on mobile. Balance click appeal with whether a reader would feel comfortable forwarding it. A promised guide, template, or complete explanation must exist in the article.

## Xiaohongshu

Put the phrase a user would naturally search in the first half when possible. The title should complement the cover rather than duplicate it. Keep the friend-to-friend tone and avoid engagement bait.

## X Short Post

Start with a fact, action, or judgment. Delete empty setup such as “Something interesting happened” or “First, the conclusion.” A neutral version still needs a concrete subject and action; a row of numbers is not a sentence.

## X Article

Make the title understandable outside the author's account. When both X formats are used, let the short post carry the vivid detail and the article title carry the whole-piece judgment.

## Douyin and Channels

The title must agree with the opening visual, spoken line, or on-screen text. Do not use a question the video never resolves. Use a more restrained tone for Channels when familiar-network sharing matters.

## Cross-Platform Rule

Do not write one title and trim it to several lengths. Change the angle because the reader state and visible surface change. Keep at least one evergreen structural title when the set also contains short-lived event or product keywords.
