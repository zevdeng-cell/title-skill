# Attention-First Hooks

Use this mode when the user wants titles that feel sensational, provocative, viral, controversial, dramatic, or deliberately clickbait-like.

## Default Move

Do not summarize the article. Find the part that creates the strongest urge to know more, then enlarge its emotional meaning while keeping checkable details real.

Use these levers aggressively:

- **Loss:** what money, time, control, opportunity, or reputation the reader could lose
- **Threat:** what breaks, fails, gets delayed, or becomes expensive
- **Contrarian judgment:** why the accepted approach is wrong, naïve, or backwards
- **Hidden reality:** what vendors, experts, competitors, or the industry rarely say plainly
- **Conflict:** design vs. manufacturing, speed vs. control, prototype vs. factory reality
- **Curiosity gap:** reveal the consequence but withhold the mechanism or answer
- **Direct accusation:** “Your supplier is not your partner” or “Your BOM is already too expensive”
- **Time pressure:** before you build, before tooling, before the deposit, before the first order
- **Editorial superlative:** biggest mistake, hardest lesson, expensive lie, brutal truth, only question that matters—when clearly presented as judgment rather than measured fact

## Useful Shapes

- `The [costly/brutal/hidden] truth about [topic]`
- `Why [ordinary action] is quietly [bad consequence]`
- `[Topic]: What [audience] learn too late`
- `Before you [commitment], read this`
- `Your [asset/process] is already [problem]—here is why`
- `The mistake that turns [stage] into [loss]`
- `[Authority/industry] will not tell you this about [topic]`
- `Stop [common action] until you answer this question`

## Calibration

Prefer a title that creates an emotional prediction over one that lists the article sections. “The Decisions That Get Expensive After Design Starts” is usually stronger than “Scope, Price, and Manufacturing Decisions.”

The body only needs to justify the direction of the judgment, not repeat the headline word for word. “Brutal truth” can summarize several documented problems. It cannot stand in for an invented failure, loss amount, quote, or event.

When asked for several options, make most of them Punchy or Max. Include a restrained SEO version only as a fallback, not as the automatic recommendation.
